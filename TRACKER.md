# Learning progress tracker

All stories begin **Not started**. A generated curriculum is not completed learner work. Change a status only when you have the relevant evidence. You may copy this file into your personal `astra/work/<your-name>/` directory; if copied, update relative links or keep this original as the navigation reference.

Statuses: **Not started**, **In progress**, **In review**, **Done**, **Blocked**, **Deferred elective**. Keep one story in progress. For Done, record the artifact/commit, actual verification, and review or teach-back. For Blocked, record a reproducible blocker and next action. Mark an unchosen elective Deferred elective rather than Done.
